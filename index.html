Questions

Questions & answers

What kind of game is Kuleru?

Kuleru: Beyond The Gates is a hand-drawn 2D action-adventure with deliberate, souls-like combat and interconnected, metroidvania-style worlds. Battle, explore, and uncover the secrets of a broken spirit realm.

When does it release?

The release date has not been announced yet.

Is there a demo?

Yes — a free demo is available now, so you can step through the gates before release. Just hit Download and pick a build.

What platforms is it on?

The demo is available for Windows 10 (64-bit). MacBook / macOS support is coming soon.

How difficult is it?

It's a deliberate, souls-like challenge. Enemies and bosses each have their own abilities and patterns — study them, then strike. The difficulty is part of the journey.

Which languages are supported?

English, Spanish (Spain), Greek, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, and Turkish.

Who is making it?

Kuleru is a single-player action-adventure developed by ALUMNI.
